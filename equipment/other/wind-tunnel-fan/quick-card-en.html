SAFETY
• Keep hands, hair and loose items away from the fan
• Never above 50 Hz (blades and bearings)
• E-STOP (red mushroom) cuts power to the drive
• After power-off: wait 5 min before opening the drive

A. Start

§5
  1. Tunnel and fan guard clear, nothing loose in the inlet.
  2. Speed knob at minimum, RUN switch off.
  3. E-stop released, press START (drive gets power, display on, no fault).
  4. RUN switch on.
  5. Turn the speed knob up slowly.

B. Stop

§5
  1. Turn the knob down, RUN switch off (fan ramps down in ~20 s).
  2. Emergency: press E-STOP. After reset the fan does not restart until RUN is switched off and on again.
  3. After use: E-stop in or mains off.

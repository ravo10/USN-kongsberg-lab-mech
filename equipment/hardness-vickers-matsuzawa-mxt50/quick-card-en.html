Matsuzawa MXT50 – Vickers HV1

Full guide with photos: scan the QR code or go to ravo10.github.io/USN-kongsberg-lab-mech. § = section in the guide.
SAFETY
• Diamond and objective must never touch the sample
• START only when the diamond has clicked in
• Do not turn the turret while LOADING is lit
• Raise the stage very slowly towards the diamond

A. Start-up

§3
  1. ON/OFF on the right side → ON.
  2. Flashlight on, push it fully into the light tube (left side).
  3. Shutter pin on the right side of the head pushed IN.
  4. HV lamp lit green (if not, press HV/HK).
  5. Load dial: 1 Kgf at the pin.
  6. DWELL TIME → 15 s, hold SET 2SEC for 2 s.

B. Zero the lines

§4A
  1. Close the lines with the right knob until only a thin line of light is left.
  2. Hold SET 2SEC for 2 s → d1 = 0.

D. Check on the HMV700 block

§4E
Make 3 indents on the block (steps 9–16). Expected diagonal ≈ 51 µm.
✓ Mean 665–735 HV → the machine is OK.
✗ Outside → zero the lines, check load and dwell time, measure again.
✗ Still outside → tell the lab supervisor.

Calculation

§6
HV = 1.8544 × F / d²
d = (d1 + d2) / 2 in mm, F in kgf. Example: d1 = 51.5 and d2 = 50.7 µm → d = 0.0511 mm → 710 HV1.
Report as e.g. 450 HV1 (mean of 3, with standard deviation).

C. One indent

§4B–D, §5
  1. 1–2 support blocks in the V-block holder, sample on top, polished face up.
  2. Orange objective (click): find and focus. Raise the stage slowly until the surface is sharp.
  3. Clean spot with X/Y: ≥ 0.5 mm from edges and old indents.
  4. Diamond (click). Raise the stage very slowly until the tip almost touches (look from the side) → START → wait for LOADING to go off.
  5. Lower the stage first (objectives reach lower than the diamond), then green objective and focus. Orange reads only ~1/4.
  6. d1: lines on the left and right corners → READ.
  7. Turn the eyepiece 90°. d2: top and bottom corners → READ → HV under DATA.
  8. Note d1, d2, HV. Next indent: one turn of the X micrometer (0.5 mm).

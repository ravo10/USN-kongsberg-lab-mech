DO NOT PRINT PETG! PETG can bond permanently to the PEI sheet and damage it.
SAFETY
• Nozzle and plate get very hot
• Do not leave during PID tuning
• IPA: cold plate only, wear gloves
• "Allow unhomed moves": crash risk

A. Before printing

§2, §3.4
  1. Print from the SD card (slot under the screen).
  2. PEI sheet clean? If prints do not stick: IPA on a cold plate.
  3. Filament loaded. Using one head only: disable extruder 2 in Cura.
  4. Start the print from the screen or Duet Web Control.

B. Cura 5.3+ (Machine Settings)

§3.1
  1. Z (Height): 630 mm.
  2. Start G-code: G28 / M566 E3600 / M572 D0 S0.02.
  3. End G-code: only M0 ; (delete the rest).
  4. 2 extruders; Right Extruder: Cooling Fan Number 1.
  5. Nozzle: 1.2 mm Nozzle.

E. After printing

§3.6
☐ Turn off the plate heating so the print comes loose
☐ Remove leftover plastic from the plate
☐ Clean up after yourself

Stepcraft M700 – CNC mill

UCCNC · SolidWorks CAM · § = section in the user manual
Full user manual with photos: scan the QR code or go to ravo10.github.io/USN-kongsberg-lab-mech.
SAFETY
• Do not use the machine without training
• Emergency stops outside and inside the door
• Clamps ≥ 50 mm outside the milling area
• Use at most 640 × 480 mm of the table

A. Start-up

§3.01
  1. Turn on the compressed air.
  2. Turn on the vacuum (switch above the mill).
  3. Release the emergency stops (out and in).
  4. Start UCCNC on the PC.
  5. Turn on the spindle (flashes blue/red).
  6. Click Reset bottom right.

B. Z zero point with the TLS

§4.07
  1. Clamp the stock.
  2. M6 → the tool used first.
  3. Move above a high point of the stock.
  4. Put the TLS on the stock, under the tool.
  5. Page Up/Down to just above the TLS.
  6. Press TLS in UCCNC and wait.
Each time the stock has a new thickness. X/Y: zero in UCCNC or use the probe.

F. Shutdown

§3.08
☐ Program finished, spindle stopped
☐ Tool in the magazine (M6), home position
☐ Spindle off, UCCNC closed
☐ Emergency stops in (outside and inside)
☐ Vacuum and compressed air off
☐ Vacuum the table and the tool holder

C. Tools and magazine

§4.01–4.05
• DOUBLE HOME ALL → OFFSETS to calibrate the tools.
• Tools 1–8. No. 9 is disabled, no. 10 = 3D Touch Probe. Change with M6 + no.
• Tool 1 is the Z reference for the probe.
• Stuck tool: press in the chuck lock, turn with the spanner.
• Keep the chuck clean: vacuum it during service.

D. DXF → G-code in UCCNC

§3.05
  1. DXF layers: no. 1 = 0, no. 2 = 11, then 12, 13 … (clean up in QCAD).
  2. CAM tab → Import DXF File → choose the layer.
  3. Choose the origin and the Operation type (inside/outside).
  4. Generate Toolpath → Create G-code, one layer at a time.

E. For students (SolidWorks)

§2
• Origin at the centre of a circle or rectangle (the probe can find it again).
• Z up, X/Y as the part lies in the machine.
• The part must fit inside 640 × 480 mm minus the clamps.
• Pocket (Finish) recommended, with tabs if needed.
• Holes under Ø6 mm: separate sketch with Ø2 mm holes.
